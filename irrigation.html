Drip Irrigation

Drip irrigation delivers water slowly and directly to the plant roots through a network of tubes. It is the most water-efficient method and widely used in greenhouses and modern farms. It helps save water while supporting healthy plant growth.

Sprinkler Irrigation

Sprinkler irrigation works like artificial rainfall, spraying water over plants with the help of sprinklers. It is suitable for vegetables and areas with sandy soil. Compared to surface irrigation, it reduces water loss and ensures more uniform coverage.

Subsurface Irrigation

This method uses underground pipes buried below the soil to deliver water directly to the root zone. It greatly reduces evaporation and water loss. Though expensive to install, it is very effective in arid regions and for high-value crops.

Sprinkler Irrigation

This is one of the simplest and oldest irrigation methods. Water is applied directly over the soil surface and allowed to flow to reach the crops. Although easy to implement, it consumes a large amount of water and is mostly used for grains like wheat and barley.
